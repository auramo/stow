# Privacy Policy for Stow

_Last updated: 13 September 2026_

Stow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What Stow stores

Your base lists, packing lists, and their items are stored **on your device
only**, in the app's own local database. They are never sent to the developer
or to any third party.

## What Stow does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No third-party SDKs or services
- No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles
- No network requests of any kind

## iCloud sync

A future version of Stow may offer optional iCloud sync. If it does, your
lists are stored in **your own private iCloud database**, readable only by
you through your Apple Account. The developer has no access to that data, and
it is not shared with anyone else. Apple's handling of it is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device. If iCloud
sync is ever enabled and you use it, you can additionally remove the app's
data from your iCloud account in iOS Settings.

## Children

Stow is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects
none from children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at
<https://github.com/auramo/stow/issues>.
